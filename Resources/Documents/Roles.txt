SuperAdmin
	* Human Role
	* Full system access (Can access any restricted feature)
	* Manages other admin accounts
	* Typically limited to 2-3 people in an organization

Administrator
	* Human Role
	* Day-to-day system management
	* Configuration changes
	* Audit log access

Operator
	* Human Role
	* Monitors system health
	* Executes routine maintenance tasks
	* Restarts services/processes
	* Limited configuration access

LinkAPI
	* System Role
	* Access granted to external links (e.g. ESignal dll link)
	* Access to limited information such as Prices, some studies
	
Bot
	* System Role
	* Access granted to Trade Bots

SVC
	* System Role
	* Access by FIG services to enable intercommunication
